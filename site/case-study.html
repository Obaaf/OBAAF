Written 2026-11-13

Expectrail, the first adopter, is one author's own project.

Osita Sean Keluo-Udeke maintains Expectrail with Timothy Itodo. This page is no evidence that a team with no tie to OBAAF finds it useful. Results from outside teams are still to come, and none has been reported.

The source is the case study. The numbers below are that document's.

What that document says worked

  • Expectrail declared its agent jobs, and the checker then asked what each could reach. Without the declaration file, the document says OBAAF recognises 2 of 19 as agent jobs.
  • Jobs that use a model key name one environment, main-only, limited to main.
  • The gate on main, from Expectrail's #223 at 17:36 UTC on 2026-11-10, pinned OBAAF to d014b63. It caught an agent job that reached main without baseline entries. The document says main went red about three hours later. Nothing was undone: the findings were accepted.

What it says did not

  • Each finding the gate raised was answered by accepting it. The baseline went from 32 to 38 entries in 21 hours. Every entry was OBAAF-GHA-005 or OBAAF-GHA-020. The document says OBAAF itself offered no fix for either at the pin Expectrail runs. It also says that pin's checker clears OBAAF-GHA-020 for a blocking Harden-Runner step, and that Expectrail has not adopted one.
  • The six incidents the work began from were, as reported, in GitHub's settings, which that pin did not read. The list of six is the account the case study started from, and the document says where the public record supports each one.
  • Nothing OBAAF ran restricted a job at run time. It reported.

What is newer than that page

The case study says an egress control exists and the checker does not credit it yet. That sentence is the page as written on 2026-11-13. The development checker, which is not in a release, clears OBAAF-GHA-020 for GitHub's native egress firewall in enforce mode, under conditions the rules page lists. The development checker can clear OBAAF-GHA-020 when the job meets the egress conditions and pins Obaaf/OBAAF/actions/egress@80db1e68769165c9ecf24e553254fa35d6e168d5, a commit on the development branch, not a release. A recognised upload alone preserves the producer’s egress verdict. OBAAF-GHA-027 reports visible execution of the downloaded files in the consuming job. Expectrail’s recorded checker pin leaves OBAAF-GHA-005 open. The development checker clears it only when all five controls verify. Release 0.3.0, like the pin, drops OBAAF-GHA-020 for a Harden-Runner step with egress-policy: block and no if, without looking at its version, its position or its sudo setting. The development checker asks for more, and answers "cannot tell" at best for Harden-Runner. The rules page records the 2026-11-14 runs.

The case study also gives the result of the settings audit, obaaf audit, which is development only. Expectrail's settings were read without a token on 2026-11-13 at 09:10 UTC and compared with its files at commit da06323: four medium findings, and seven rules that could not tell. On that same reading and those same files, the development audit of 2026-11-17 finds five medium findings, and the same seven rules cannot tell. The new finding is about main-only. Only main may use that environment, and it has no required reviewers. But on that reading, merging into main needed no one's approval, only the required checks, so anyone with write access could merge a pull request that adds a job using the environment. These counts are for the reading of 2026-11-13; the settings may have changed since.

On 2026-11-17 Expectrail's main, at commit 791f50b, still installed OBAAF at commit d014b63, of 2026-11-10, as the case study records. The later work does not apply to it until that pin moves.

Expectrail first ran the checker, in reporting mode, on 2026-10-30. The gate command the document quotes is in the block below.

obaaf check . --baseline .github/obaaf-baseline.json --fail-on low --no-fixes
